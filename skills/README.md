# Skills

This is the canonical home for the Radarboard extension-creation skills.

Rules:

- Keep `create-integration`, `create-plugin`, and `create-widget` here.
- Do not keep separate copies of those three under `.agents/skills` or `.claude/skills`.
- Mirror those three into `.agents/skills` and `.claude/skills` with per-skill symlinks.
- General shared skills can stay in `.agents/skills` and be mirrored into `.claude/skills` as needed.
